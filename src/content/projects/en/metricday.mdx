## The problem

Teams track time in one tool and manage OKRs in another — two worlds that never talk. The result: hours logged with no idea whether they move strategic goals forward, and OKRs reviewed at quarter end without execution data.

## The approach

Metricday connects daily execution to goals: every timed task is attached to a Key Result. A unified workspace brings intuitive timers, OKR alignment, a performance dashboard with visualizations, and smart deadline reminders — without juggling tools.

## Where it stands

- Product in Early Access with an open waitlist
- Designed and built solo, from visual identity to deployment
- Landing page and app built with Next.js
